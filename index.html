Having trouble joining?

This almost always means Step 1 was skipped or done with a different Google account.

  1. Go back to Step 1 and join the Google Group.
  2. Make sure your browser and your Play Store app are signed into the same Google account.
  3. Wait up to 15 minutes after joining, then try the opt-in link again.
  4. If still stuck, try opening the opt-in link in Chrome (not a third-party browser).
Open the Google Play Store app → tap your profile picture (top-right) → your active account email is shown at the top of that panel. Use that exact email when joining the Google Group.

Google's propagation can take up to 15–30 minutes after joining the group.

Also try: clearing Play Store cache (Settings → Apps → Google Play Store → Storage → Clear Cache), or switching to your mobile data connection and retrying.

Still stuck? Email us directly and we'll add your account manually.
